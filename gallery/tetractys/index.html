Tetractys

Posted on Tue 20 August 2024 in gallery

Tetractys is a LED art installation inspired by the ancient Pythagorean symbol of the same name. The Tetractys, meaning "fourness," is a triangular figure consisting of ten points arranged in four rows, representing the fundamental principles of harmony and cosmic order in Pythagorean philosophy.

This piece translates the sacred geometry of the Tetractys into a three-dimensional LED sculpture, where each point of the original triangle becomes a glowing sphere. The LEDs are programmed to create flowing patterns that follow the mathematical relationships inherent in the Tetractys - the ratios of 1:2:3:4 that were considered fundamental to musical harmony and cosmic order.

The installation explores the intersection of ancient wisdom and modern technology, using contemporary LED art to illuminate timeless mathematical and philosophical concepts. The light patterns cycle through different sequences that correspond to musical intervals and harmonic ratios, creating a visual representation of the "music of the spheres" that the Pythagoreans believed governed the universe.

The piece invites viewers to contemplate the deep connections between mathematics, music, art, and the fundamental structure of reality itself.

Medium: LEDs, Electronics, Steel Frame, Glass Spheres
Dimensions: 36" x 36" x 18"
Year: 2024
Philosophical Inspiration: Pythagorean Tetractys
Programming: Harmonic ratio-based light sequences
Power: 12V DC, 75W
