Melty Cube

Posted on Sun 15 September 2024 in gallery

Melty Cube is an interactive LED art installation that explores the concept of digital decay and transformation. This geometric sculpture features a cube structure with LED strips that create flowing, organic patterns that seem to "melt" and reform in response to viewer interaction.

The piece incorporates proximity sensors and accelerometers that detect movement and touch, causing the LED patterns to shift and evolve in real-time. The visual effects simulate liquid metal or digital matter flowing through the rigid geometric structure, creating a fascinating contrast between the mathematical precision of the cube and the organic fluidity of the light patterns.

Each face of the cube displays different algorithmic patterns - some inspired by cellular automata, others by fluid dynamics simulations. The interactive elements allow viewers to become part of the artwork, their movements influencing the flow and intensity of the light patterns.

The installation explores themes of transformation, the intersection of digital and physical worlds, and the ephemeral nature of light and perception.

Medium: LEDs, Electronics, Acrylic, Sensors
Dimensions: 24" x 24" x 24"
Year: 2024
Interactive Elements: Proximity sensors, Accelerometer
Power: 24V DC, 100W
