Fractious Flow

Posted on Tue 01 October 2024 in gallery

Fractious Flow is a mesmerizing kinetic LED art piece inspired by the organic forms of sea urchins and the hypnotic patterns found in nature. This 32-inch spinning sculpture was created specifically for SOAK 2024, showcasing the intersection of technology and organic beauty.

The piece features hundreds of individually addressable LEDs arranged in a radial pattern that creates stunning POV (Persistence of Vision) effects when spinning. As the sculpture rotates, intricate patterns emerge and dissolve, creating a dynamic visual experience that changes with each rotation.

The design draws inspiration from the Fibonacci spiral and natural growth patterns, creating a sense of both mathematical precision and organic flow. The LED programming creates waves of light that cascade across the surface, mimicking the movement of water and the pulsing rhythm of marine life.

Medium: LEDs, Electronics, Steel Frame
Dimensions: 32" x 32" x 32"
Year: 2024
Location: SOAK 2024
Power: 12V DC, 50W
